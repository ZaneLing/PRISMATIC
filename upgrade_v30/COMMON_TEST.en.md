# Common frozen ATC-L4 method comparison

## Protocol and interpretation

Exactly 2,422 test visits from 890 patients, with separate 271-class full and 219-class molecular panels. All methods share source partition, observed inputs, case order and the within-panel label space. The source mapping covers 27.23% of selected prescription rows; these are retrospective mapped-class sets, not complete prospective prescriptions. Known earlier test-patient overlap makes this exploratory. No paper-reported score is mixed into the executed table.

v29/v30 fit policies on 5,692 patients and reserve 1,423 for rule verification. v27 methods follow their published adaptations and registered budgets. Exposure to the source training pool is shared, while optimization and API/FLOP budgets differ. v30 reuses the exact v29 fits. All thresholds, rules and checkpoint choices freeze before this evaluation.
